Sync from Apple Health

Steps, calories burned and weight from Google Health (Fitbit) through Apple Health.

  1. In the Google Health app (it syncs your Fitbit), open Connections → Apple Health, tap Get started and allow steps, active calories and weight. In Apple's Health app you can check that Steps, Active Energy and Weight have data.
  2. Open Shortcuts, tap + and name the shortcut MacroFlow Health.
  3. Add Find Health Samples. Set Type is Steps, Start Date is in the last 7 days, Group By: Day, Sort by: Start Date. Allow access when asked.
  4. Add Repeat with Each (it repeats over the Health Samples). Inside it, add a Text action containing:
    steps; then the Repeat Item variable — tap it, choose Start Date, and set the date format to ISO 8601 — then ; then Repeat Item → Value, a space, and Repeat Item → Unit.
  5. Do steps 3–4 again for Active Energy (text starts with active;) and Weight (weight;). If you wear an Apple Watch, add Resting Energy (resting;) too — Fitbit doesn't send it, so MacroFlow estimates it from your profile. You can long-press the first Find/Repeat pair to duplicate it.
  6. After the last End Repeat, add a Text action: type MacroFlow Health v1 on the first line, then insert each Repeat Results on its own line.
  7. Add Copy to Clipboard (it copies that Text).
  8. Make it automatic: in Shortcuts open Automation → + → Time of Day, pick a time, choose Run Immediately and select MacroFlow Health. Add a few (for example 7 am, 1 pm and 9 pm). Each run copies fresh data, replacing whatever you had copied.
  9. Open MacroFlow and tap Sync Apple Health, then Paste when your iPhone asks. iOS needs this one tap: websites can't read Apple Health or the clipboard on their own.

The copied text looks like this

MacroFlow Health v1 steps;2026-10-15T00:00:00-04:00;8432 count active;2026-10-15T00:00:00-04:00;512.3 kcal resting;2026-10-15T00:00:00-04:00;1750 kcal weight;2026-10-15T00:00:00-04:00;78.4 kg

Active energy is logged as exercise for that day. Total burned is active + resting energy (resting is estimated from your Settings profile when Apple Health has none, as Fitbit does) and shows as energy balance on Insights. Steps are shown on Today and weights go to Progress. Syncing again replaces the synced days, never your own entries; days with weigh-ins you entered yourself keep your number.
